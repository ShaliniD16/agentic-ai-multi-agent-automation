# Algebra Problem Solution

## Problem Statement:
Evaluate the expression: **3 * 4 + 3 = ?**

---

## Step-by-Step Explanation:

1. **Understand Order of Operations (PEMDAS / BODMAS):**
   When solving expressions with multiple operations, follow the standard order of operations:
   - **P**arentheses / **B**rackets
   - **E**xponents / **O**rders
   - **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision (from left to right)
   - **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction (from left to right)

2. **Step 1: Perform Multiplication**
   In the expression `3 * 4 + 3`, multiplication takes priority over addition.
   Calculate:
   $$3 \times 4 = 12$$

3. **Step 2: Perform Addition**
   Now substitute `12` back into the expression:
   $$12 + 3 = 15$$

---

## Final Result:
**3 * 4 + 3 = 15**
